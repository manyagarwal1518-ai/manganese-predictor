i About the Prediction System

The Manganese Reserve Predictor is a flagship initiative of the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, developed under the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) programme. The system utilises Artificial Intelligence and multi-spectral satellite imagery to accurately estimate manganese mineral reserves and forecast potential production shortfalls.


By analysing geological formations, remote-sensing spectral data, and historical mining production records, the predictor delivers actionable insights for government planners, industry professionals, and policymakers to optimise resource planning and supply-chain management at a national scale.

★ Key Features & Capabilities

🛰️ Satellite Data Integration
Processes multi-spectral data from 18 active satellites including IRS and Sentinel series.
🤖 AI-based Forecasting
Deep learning models trained on 10+ years of geological survey and production data.
🗺️ Geospatial Mapping
GIS-integrated reserve maps covering all major manganese-bearing zones in India.
📊 Shortfall Analysis
Identifies production gaps and flags critical shortage risk 6–18 months in advance.
📄 Automated Reporting
Generates ministry-ready reports and policy briefs in standard GOI formats.
🔒 Secure & Compliant
Hosted on MeitY-approved cloud infrastructure with data localisation compliance.

📍 Major Manganese Reserve Zones Covered

  • Odisha – Keonjhar, Sundargarh, Jajpur (Largest reserves in India)
  • Maharashtra – Nagpur, Bhandara, Gondia districts
  • Madhya Pradesh – Balaghat (High-grade ore deposits)
  • Andhra Pradesh – Srikakulam, Vizianagaram belt
  • Karnataka – Sandur, Tumkur manganese belt
  • Goa – Bicholim, Sattari and Ponda regions
  • Rajasthan – Banswara and Dungarpur zones
